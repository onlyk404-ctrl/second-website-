Websites start at ₹9,999, apps at ₹24,999 and AI solutions at ₹19,999. Final price depends on features & timeline — message me your idea and I'll share a free, honest estimate within 24 hours.

How long will my project take?

A landing page takes ~1 week, a full website 2–3 weeks, a mobile app 4–6 weeks, and an AI chatbot 2–4 weeks. You'll get a clear timeline before we start — and regular updates while I build.

Yes! I work remotely with clients worldwide. I'm flexible with time zones, communicate in English, and share progress daily over WhatsApp, email or video calls.

Absolutely. Every project includes free support for 30 days after launch — bug fixes, small changes & guidance. Affordable monthly maintenance plans are available too.
